About

I'm a software developer from Santiago, Chile, currently studying Information Technology at Ensign College with a specialization in AI development. Most of my work so far has been freelance: building inventory systems for small businesses, adding AI-driven automation to help owners manage their data, and designing the networks that connect a company's branch offices into one picture.

I'm also a disciple of Jesus Christ, and I've stopped treating that as separate from my technical work. The same principle shows up everywhere I look in this field: a system is only as good as how well its parts are connected — and I've come to believe the same is true of a life.

Guiding principles

Three threads, woven together rather than run side by side.

Diligent work as an act of worship

Careful, honest work on a client's system is not separate from my discipleship — it's one of the most repeatable ways I practice it, whether or not anyone but God notices the difference.

Continuous learning as a disciple's duty

AI development moves faster than almost any field I could have chosen. I've come to see staying current not as a career requirement, but as stewardship over the talents I've been given.

Trust in God through uncertainty

Building AI tools means sitting with a real fear — that the systems I help create might replace the jobs of the people they're meant to help. I can't resolve that by ignoring it. I can build with integrity, and trust the rest.
